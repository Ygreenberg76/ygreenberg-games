MY REVIEW • 0.933 HOURS PLAYED

The Lab

ActionSingleplayerAdventure
7/10

What I liked

I liked trying the different VR experiences and seeing how interaction and movement work in virtual reality.

What I didn't like

It feels more like a collection of VR experiments and demonstrations than a complete game.

Combat & Gameplay

The different activities are useful for experiencing what VR can do, and some of them are entertaining even though they're fairly simple.

Final Thoughts

A fun VR experience and a good demonstration of the technology, but there isn't enough depth for me to treat it like a full game.
