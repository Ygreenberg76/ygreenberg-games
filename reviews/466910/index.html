MY REVIEW • 22.1 HOURS PLAYED

Worm.is: The Game

SingleplayerActionAdventure
6/10

What I liked

It's simple and easy to understand. You grow your worm, avoid other players and try to get bigger. It can be entertaining when you just want something quick to play.

What I didn't like

There isn't enough variety to keep me interested for a long time. Once you've played enough matches, you've basically seen what the game has to offer.

Combat & Gameplay

Trying to trap other players while avoiding getting trapped yourself can be fun, especially when you've built a large worm and don't want to lose everything.

Final Thoughts

A simple game that can be fun for a while, but there isn't enough depth or variety for me to rate it higher.
