MY REVIEW • 2.7 HOURS PLAYED

Half-Life 2

ShooterActionAdventure
8/10

What I liked

I liked how the game combines shooting, exploration, physics and environmental puzzles instead of constantly giving you the same type of combat.

What I didn't like

Some parts feel dated now, especially compared with newer shooters, and there are sections where the pacing slows down.

Story

The game doesn't constantly stop to explain everything. You learn about City 17, the Combine and what happened to the world while continuing to play.

Combat & Gameplay

The Gravity Gun is easily one of the most interesting parts. Being able to use objects from the environment as weapons makes combat much more creative.

Final Thoughts

A very good FPS with creative gameplay and excellent use of physics. It obviously shows its age in some areas, but I can understand why it became such an important game.
