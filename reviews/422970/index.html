MY REVIEW • PLAYTIME NOT RECORDED

Devil Daggers

Story RichActionSingleplayer
6/10

What I liked

The gameplay is extremely fast, and the simple objective makes it easy to understand immediately.

What I didn't like

The experience is very repetitive and focused on surviving and improving your score rather than giving you a story or larger progression system.

Combat & Gameplay

Constant movement and fast reactions are basically everything. The challenge comes from surviving longer as more enemies appear.

Final Thoughts

It's good at providing a fast and difficult arcade-style challenge, but it's not really the type of game that gives me enough variety to keep playing for a long time.
