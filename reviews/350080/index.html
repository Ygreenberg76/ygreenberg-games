MY REVIEW • 10.6 HOURS PLAYED

Wolfenstein: The Old Blood

ShooterStealthStory Rich
7/10

What I liked

The shooting feels good, and I liked returning to the Wolfenstein world for another shorter adventure.

What I didn't like

It doesn't feel as developed as the main Wolfenstein games. The shorter length also means there isn't as much time for the story or characters to develop.

Story

The story works as a prequel and gives B.J. another mission before the events of The New Order, but the action was more important to me than the story.

Combat & Gameplay

The weapons are powerful and the combination of stealth and direct combat gives you some choice in how you approach enemies.

Final Thoughts

A good, shorter Wolfenstein experience with enjoyable shooting, but it doesn't have the same impact as the bigger games in the series.
