MY REVIEW • PLAYTIME NOT RECORDED

SiN Gold

Open WorldShooterAction
7/10

What I liked

It has the fast action and level design of classic PC shooters, with plenty of weapons and enemies.

What I didn't like

Its age is very noticeable today in the graphics, controls and general presentation.

Combat & Gameplay

It's much more about moving through levels, finding weapons and shooting enemies than modern progression systems or open-world mechanics.

Final Thoughts

A good example of an older FPS. I can appreciate what it does, but its age makes it harder for me to enjoy as much as a modern shooter. 7/10.
