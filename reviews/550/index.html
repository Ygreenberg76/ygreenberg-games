MY REVIEW • 0.583 HOURS PLAYED

Left 4 Dead 2

ShooterSurvivalAction
7/10

What I liked

The large groups of infected make the action fast and chaotic. Having different special infected also keeps fights from being completely predictable.

What I didn't like

The game is designed heavily around cooperative play, while I normally prefer playing games by myself.

Combat & Gameplay

The shooting is simple and fast, and dealing with large numbers of infected can become intense very quickly.

Final Thoughts

I only played for a short time, but I can understand why it's such a popular co-op zombie shooter. The gameplay is fun; it's just more multiplayer-focused than what I normally look for.
