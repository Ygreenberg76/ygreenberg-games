MY REVIEW • 0.783 HOURS PLAYED

Crab Game

MultiplayerSingleplayerAction
5/10

What I liked

The mini-games can be funny and chaotic, especially when a lot of players are competing at the same time.

What I didn't like

The gameplay didn't hold my attention for very long. It feels more like something to play for a few laughs than a game I would spend many hours playing.

Combat & Gameplay

The simple challenges are easy to understand, and most of the entertainment comes from the unpredictable things that happen with other players.

Final Thoughts

Funny for a short time, but there wasn't enough depth to keep me interested. Based on my short time with it, it's a 5 for me.
