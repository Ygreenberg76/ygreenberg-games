MY REVIEW • 6.6 HOURS PLAYED

Remember Me

Story RichActionAdventure
6/10

What I liked

The futuristic setting and the idea of manipulating memories are interesting. Neo-Paris also gives the game a distinctive look.

What I didn't like

The game is very linear, and I didn't feel like I had enough freedom to explore the world. Combat also started becoming repetitive.

Story

The idea that memories can be stolen, changed and controlled is probably the most interesting part of the story.

Combat & Gameplay

The combo system lets you customize attacks, which is a good idea, but I didn't find the combat interesting enough to keep me fully engaged.

Final Thoughts

Remember Me has an interesting world and some creative ideas, but the linear gameplay and repetitive combat kept it at a 6 for me.
