MY REVIEW • PLAYTIME NOT RECORDED

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition

RPGStory RichAction
7/10

What I liked

The characters, story choices and RPG systems give the game a lot of depth, and your decisions are an important part of the experience.

What I didn't like

The combat and presentation feel dated today, and the slower tactical style isn't always the type of combat I prefer.

Story

The story and characters are stronger parts of the experience for me than the actual combat.

Comparison

I rated my other Dragon Age: Origins entry 8.5/10. I'm keeping this separate Ultimate Edition entry at the 7/10 score I chose rather than treating the two Steam entries as the same rating.

Final Thoughts

A deep RPG with strong characters and choices, but this particular entry gets a 7 from me.
