MY REVIEW • 1.133 HOURS PLAYED

Counter-Strike 2

ShooterMultiplayerSingleplayer
7/10

What I liked

The shooting is very skill-based, and every mistake can matter. Winning a fight because you reacted faster or aimed better can be satisfying.

What I didn't like

It's heavily focused on competitive multiplayer, which isn't really the type of gaming experience I normally prefer.

Combat & Gameplay

Weapons require accuracy and practice, and you can't simply run around shooting like you can in some faster arcade shooters.

Final Thoughts

A solid competitive shooter with very skill-based gunplay. I understand the appeal, but competitive multiplayer isn't enough to keep me playing for a long time.
