MY REVIEW • 0.633 HOURS PLAYED

Plants vs. Zombies: Game of the Year

SurvivalStrategySingleplayer
8/10

What I liked

The concept is simple but extremely well done. Choosing the right plants and figuring out where to place them makes the gameplay surprisingly entertaining.

What I didn't like

I only have a short amount of recorded playtime on Steam, so I didn't experience everything this version of the game offers.

Combat & Gameplay

Different plants have different purposes, and deciding how to build your defenses as new zombies appear is what makes the game fun.

Final Thoughts

A simple game that is very easy to understand but still gives you enough strategy to keep things interesting. Even from my limited Steam playtime, I enjoyed it enough to give it an 8.
