REVIEW IN PROGRESS • 26 HOURS PLAYED

Hell Is Us

Action AdventureExplorationMystery
6.5/10

Provisional rating — I am still playing, and this score may change.

What I Liked

The graphics are good, and the game has become more interesting as I've continued playing. You really need to look at everything, study what you find, and listen carefully to what everyone says. The puzzles are based on what you've learned and understood.

What I Didn't Like

The combat is not fun at all. The drone and some abilities are exceptions, but regular fighting is boring. If I weren't playing this game to write a review, I would have stopped already and moved on to another game.

Story

The story is okay, with some interesting elements, but it hasn't been what keeps me playing.

Exploration and Puzzles

To solve puzzles, you need to examine your surroundings, pay attention to conversations, and remember what you've discovered. The game rewards careful investigation rather than rushing through.

Is It Worth Buying?

I would definitely not buy Hell Is Us at full price. I'd only recommend considering it on sale, especially if you enjoy exploration and puzzles more than combat.

Current Verdict

After 26 hours, I'm leaning toward a 6.5/10. The graphics and investigative puzzles are good, but the combat seriously hurts the experience. This is still a review in progress, not my final score.
