MY REVIEW • 12.3 HOURS PLAYED

Totally Accurate Battlegrounds

SingleplayerActionAdventure
7/10

What I liked

The ridiculous physics and movement make the game funny. It doesn't try to be a serious battle royale, and that's part of what makes it entertaining.

What I didn't like

The joke can eventually wear off, and the intentionally awkward controls can sometimes become frustrating instead of funny.

Combat & Gameplay

Weapons and movement are unpredictable, which can create some very funny fights. You never really know exactly how an encounter is going to go.

Final Thoughts

A funny and different take on battle royale games. It's entertaining, especially in shorter sessions, but the crazy physics and limited depth kept it at a 7.
