MY REVIEW • 4.4 HOURS PLAYED

Darksiders II

RPGActionAdventure
7/10

What I liked

I liked the combination of combat, exploration, equipment and RPG elements. Playing as Death also gives the game a different style.

What I didn't like

Some areas and puzzles slowed the game down for me, and with only a few hours played I didn't get far enough into the game to become completely invested.

Combat & Gameplay

Combat is fast, and finding different weapons and equipment gives you reasons to keep improving your character.

Exploration & Open World

The world has dungeons, puzzles and hidden areas, although I didn't play enough to experience everything the game offers.

Final Thoughts

A good action RPG with enjoyable combat and an interesting world. I liked what I played, but I didn't spend enough time with it to rate it higher.
