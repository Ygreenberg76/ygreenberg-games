MY REVIEW • 19.8 HOURS PLAYED

Monster Jam

RacingSingleplayerAction
5/10

What I liked

Driving the monster trucks and performing jumps and stunts can be fun, especially at the beginning. The trucks themselves are the main reason I kept playing.

What I didn't like

The gameplay becomes repetitive pretty quickly. There just isn't enough variety to keep the experience interesting for a long time.

Combat & Gameplay

Driving such large trucks should feel exciting, and there are moments when it does, especially during jumps and crashes. But the overall gameplay isn't deep enough for me.

Final Thoughts

It can be fun in short sessions if you like monster trucks, but after almost 20 hours I felt like I had seen most of what the game had to offer.
