MY REVIEW • 31.7 HOURS PLAYED

Battlefield: Bad Company™ 2

ShooterStory RichAction
7/10

What I liked

The shooting and destruction are the best parts. Being able to destroy buildings and cover makes battles more interesting because the environment can actually change during a fight.

What I didn't like

For me, it doesn't have the depth or long-term appeal of the shooters I enjoy most. I had fun with it, but after around 30 hours I didn't feel a strong reason to keep going.

Story

The campaign has some fun characters and big action moments. I liked that the squad has more personality than you normally get in a military shooter.

Combat & Gameplay

Weapons feel good, vehicles add variety, and destroying the environment makes combat different from shooters where everything around you is permanent.

Final Thoughts

A good Battlefield game with fun destruction and shooting, but not one of my personal favorite shooters.
