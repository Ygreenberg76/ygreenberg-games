MY REVIEW • 105.3 HOURS PLAYED

Assassin's Creed Odyssey

RPGOpen WorldStory Rich
8/10

What I liked

Ancient Greece is beautiful and huge. I enjoyed exploring the islands, fighting enemies, upgrading equipment, and developing my character.

What I didn't like

The size is also one of its weaknesses. There is so much content that some activities and locations eventually begin to feel repetitive.

Story

The story kept me interested, and I liked having choices during conversations and missions. It helps make the adventure feel more like an RPG than some of the older Assassin's Creed games.

Combat & Gameplay

Combat is fun, especially after unlocking more abilities and finding better equipment. There are many different weapons and ways to build your character.

Exploration & Open World

The world is enormous and there is a lot to discover. Sailing between islands also gives the game a real sense of adventure.

Who I Recommend It To

Players who enjoy Assassin's Creed, RPG progression, historical worlds, and very large open-world games.

Final Thoughts

A beautiful and very large Assassin's Creed RPG with lots to explore.
