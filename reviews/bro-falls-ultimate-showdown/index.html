MY REVIEW • 9.5 HOURS PLAYED

Bro Falls: Ultimate Showdown

SingleplayerActionAdventure
7/10

What I liked

It's simple, colorful and easy to jump into. The different obstacle courses can be funny, especially when everything becomes chaotic with a lot of players trying to survive.

What I didn't like

After playing enough rounds, the basic gameplay starts becoming repetitive. There isn't enough depth to keep me interested for very long sessions.

Combat & Gameplay

The controls are simple, and most of the fun comes from trying to survive the obstacles while watching other players make mistakes.

Final Thoughts

A fun casual game for shorter sessions. I enjoyed the crazy obstacle courses, but the repetitive gameplay kept it at a 7 for me.
