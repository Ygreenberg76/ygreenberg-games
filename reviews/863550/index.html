MY REVIEW • 2.9 HOURS PLAYED

HITMAN™ 2

ShooterStealthAction
7/10

What I liked

I liked having different ways to complete an assassination. Watching the environment and finding opportunities is much more interesting than simply shooting your way to the target.

What I didn't like

With only a few hours played, I didn't spend enough time learning all the different possibilities in each location.

Combat & Gameplay

Disguises, distractions, environmental opportunities and stealth give you a lot of options. The game rewards you for taking your time and understanding how the location works.

Exploration & Open World

The levels are designed so that exploring can reveal new routes and different ways to reach or eliminate your target.

Final Thoughts

A good stealth game with a lot of freedom in how you approach your targets. I can see how much depth the levels have, but I didn't play enough of it to rate it higher.
