MY REVIEW • 45.1 HOURS PLAYED

WWE 2K25

WrestlingSingleplayerAction
8/10

What I liked

There is a lot to do, and the actual wrestling feels good. Having so many wrestlers, match types and different modes gives you plenty of ways to play.

What I didn't like

Not every mode is equally interesting, and some parts feel like they require more time than I want to spend on them.

Combat & Gameplay

The wrestling does a good job of making matches feel like WWE instead of just being a normal fighting game. Building momentum, using signature moves and hitting finishers makes a good match feel like something you could actually watch on WWE.

Exploration & Open World

There is a lot of variety. Universe and GM especially give you the ability to create your own shows, rivalries and stories, while Showcase gives you something completely different to play.

Who I Recommend It To

WWE fans who want a lot of wrestlers, match types and different modes to play.

Final Thoughts

A very good WWE game with enough content to keep a wrestling fan busy for a long time.
