MY REVIEW • PLAYTIME NOT RECORDED

Crysis Warhead

ShooterActionSingleplayer
8/10

What I liked

The combination of weapons, the nanosuit and larger environments gives you more freedom than a typical linear shooter.

What I didn't like

Some elements naturally feel dated now, especially compared with newer action games.

Combat & Gameplay

The nanosuit is what makes the combat interesting. Being able to use abilities together with weapons gives you different ways to approach fights instead of always attacking the same way.

Final Thoughts

A strong FPS with good action and more freedom than many shooters. The nanosuit helps make the gameplay stand out, and I give it an 8.
