MY REVIEW • 7.9 HOURS PLAYED

Crosshair V2

SingleplayerActionAdventure
6/10

What I liked

It's a simple utility that does what it's supposed to do by giving you a customizable crosshair on the screen.

What I didn't like

There really isn't much to it beyond that. It's useful in certain situations, but it's not something I would consider a full gaming experience.

Combat & Gameplay

It can be useful for games where the existing crosshair isn't very good or where you want something easier to see.

Final Thoughts

A useful little tool for certain games, but very limited in what it offers. It does its job, and that's basically it.
