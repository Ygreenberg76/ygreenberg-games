MY REVIEW • 26.8 HOURS PLAYED

UNCHARTED™: Legacy of Thieves Collection

Open WorldShooterStory Rich
9/10

What I liked

The story, characters, graphics and action are all excellent. The games feel like playing through a big adventure movie, with great locations and some incredible action scenes.

What I didn't like

The climbing can sometimes feel too guided because there is usually one specific route the game wants you to take.

Story

The characters are probably my favorite part. Nathan Drake, Sully, Elena and the other characters make the story entertaining even when there isn't any action happening.

Combat & Gameplay

The shooting is fun, but I especially liked how the game constantly changes between combat, climbing, puzzles and huge action sequences. It keeps the adventure moving.

Exploration & Open World

There are some beautiful environments to explore, although this isn't really an open-world game. The larger areas still give you some freedom to look around and discover optional things.

Final Thoughts

One of the best cinematic adventure games I've played. Great characters, beautiful graphics and excellent action from beginning to end.
