MY REVIEW • 1.767 HOURS PLAYED

Destiny 2

ShooterStory RichAction
7/10

What I liked

The shooting feels very smooth, and the weapons and abilities make combat satisfying even when you're simply fighting normal enemies.

What I didn't like

With less than two hours played, I didn't get deeply into the progression, story or larger amount of content. It also feels designed around spending much more time with the game than I wanted to.

Combat & Gameplay

Gunplay is definitely the strongest part from what I played. Combining weapons with character abilities makes the combat fast and enjoyable.

Exploration & Open World

The environments look good and give you different areas to fight and explore, although I only experienced a small part of what the game offers.

Final Thoughts

A good-looking shooter with excellent gunplay, but I didn't spend enough time with Destiny 2 to become invested in its larger progression and online systems.
