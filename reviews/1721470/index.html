MY REVIEW • 4.8 HOURS PLAYED

Poppy Playtime

AdventurePuzzleHorror
7/10

What I liked

The abandoned toy factory is creepy, and the game does a good job of making normally harmless-looking toys feel disturbing.

What I didn't like

There are sections where the puzzles slow the game down, and with my playtime I didn't experience enough of the game to rate it higher.

Combat & Gameplay

Exploring, solving puzzles and using the GrabPack gives you something to do besides simply running away from monsters.

Exploration & Open World

The empty factory and the feeling that something might appear at any moment are what make the game work.

Final Thoughts

A good horror game with a creepy setting and some interesting puzzle ideas. I enjoyed what I played, but not enough to put it above a 7.
