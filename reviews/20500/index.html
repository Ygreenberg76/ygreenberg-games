MY REVIEW • 1.533 HOURS PLAYED

Red Faction: Guerrilla Steam Edition

Story RichActionAdventure
7/10

What I liked

The destruction system is easily the most interesting part. Being able to actually destroy buildings and structures makes the world much more interactive than in many other action games.

What I didn't like

I only played for around an hour and a half, so I didn't get far enough to experience much of the story or progression.

Combat & Gameplay

Destroying structures with weapons and the sledgehammer is satisfying, and I liked that destruction can actually be part of how you approach an objective.

Final Thoughts

From the time I played, the destruction technology was the part that stood out the most. I enjoyed it, but I didn't play long enough to experience everything the game offers.
