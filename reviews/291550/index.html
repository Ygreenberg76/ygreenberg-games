MY REVIEW • 0.967 HOURS PLAYED

Brawlhalla

Story RichMultiplayerSingleplayer
7/10

What I liked

It's easy to start playing, and the fast matches make it a good game when you just want something quick.

What I didn't like

The basic fighting loop started feeling repetitive to me fairly quickly.

Combat & Gameplay

Different characters and weapons give the fighting some variety, while knocking opponents off the stage keeps matches simple and competitive.

Final Thoughts

A fun fighting game for shorter sessions. I enjoyed what I played, but it didn't have enough to make me want to spend a lot more time with it.
