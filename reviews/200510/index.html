MY REVIEW • PLAYTIME NOT RECORDED

XCOM: Enemy Unknown

RPGActionStrategy
6/10

What I liked

I can appreciate the amount of strategy involved. Managing your squad, choosing positions and deciding how to approach each situation gives the game a lot of tactical depth.

What I didn't like

Turn-based combat isn't really my preferred style. I prefer controlling the action directly instead of taking turns and planning every individual move.

Combat & Gameplay

There is clearly a lot of strategy involved in positioning, cover, abilities and managing your soldiers, but the slower tactical gameplay isn't a great match for me.

Final Thoughts

I understand why people who enjoy tactical games can really like XCOM, but the turn-based gameplay doesn't fit the way I normally like to play. 6/10.
