MY REVIEW • 1.2 HOURS PLAYED

Delta Force

ShooterActionSingleplayer
7/10

What I liked

The shooting feels good, and I liked the larger-scale military combat with different weapons and equipment.

What I didn't like

I only played for a little over an hour, so I didn't experience enough of the game to become invested in its progression or different modes.

Combat & Gameplay

From what I played, the gunplay is enjoyable and the larger environments give you more room to approach fights than a very linear shooter.

Final Thoughts

A good military shooter from my limited time with it. The combat was enjoyable, but I didn't play enough to give it anything higher than a 7.
