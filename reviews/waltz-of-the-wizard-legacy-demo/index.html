MY REVIEW • 0.183 HOURS PLAYED

Waltz of the Wizard (Legacy demo)

Story RichActionSingleplayer
7/10

What I liked

Playing around with magic in VR is entertaining because you can actually interact with objects and experiment with different things around you.

What I didn't like

It's more of a VR demonstration than a full game, and my playtime was extremely short.

Combat & Gameplay

The fun comes from interacting with the environment and experimenting with magical abilities rather than following a traditional story or mission structure.

Final Thoughts

A fun little VR experience that shows how well magic and interaction can work in virtual reality. There isn't much for me to judge from such a short demo, but I enjoyed what I tried.
