MY REVIEW • 0.6 HOURS PLAYED

Hitman: Sniper Challenge

ShooterStealthSingleplayer
7/10

What I liked

I liked having to observe the environment and decide when and where to take a shot instead of simply shooting every target immediately.

What I didn't like

It's a very limited experience compared with a full Hitman game, and I only spent a short amount of time with it.

Combat & Gameplay

Trying to eliminate targets without creating unnecessary attention makes the sniping more interesting than simply hitting long-distance shots.

Final Thoughts

A fun small Hitman experience that works well as a sniper challenge, but it obviously doesn't have the freedom and variety of a full Hitman game.
