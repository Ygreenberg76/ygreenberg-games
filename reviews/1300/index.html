SiN Episodes: Emergence

RPGShooterAction
8/10

What I liked

I like the fast first-person shooting and the more traditional FPS design. It doesn't try to overcomplicate the basic action.

What I didn't like

The episodic format means the experience doesn't feel as complete as a larger full-length game.

Combat & Gameplay

The focus is on straightforward FPS action with different weapons and enemies rather than complicated RPG systems or progression.

Final Thoughts

A good old-school-style FPS that gives me the kind of straightforward shooting I enjoy. 8/10.
