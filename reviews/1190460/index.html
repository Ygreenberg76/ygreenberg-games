MY REVIEW • 36.4 HOURS PLAYED

DEATH STRANDING

Story RichAdventureSingleplayer
8/10

What I liked

Death Stranding is completely different from almost anything else I've played. Planning a route, carrying cargo and figuring out how to cross difficult terrain sounds simple, but somehow it becomes satisfying.

What I didn't like

The game can be very slow. There are long sections where you're mostly traveling and delivering cargo, so you need patience to really enjoy it.

Story

The story is strange and sometimes confusing, but that also made me want to understand what was happening and why this world became the way it is.

Exploration & Open World

The environment itself is almost your enemy. Mountains, rivers and difficult terrain can turn a simple delivery into something you actually need to plan.

Building & Customization

I really liked how roads, bridges and other structures can make future trips easier. Slowly improving your routes gives you a real feeling of progress.

Final Thoughts

Definitely not a game for everyone, but it's different enough that I enjoyed the experience and I'm glad I played it.
