MY REVIEW • 13 HOURS PLAYED

Police Simulator: Patrol Officers

ShooterActionSingleplayer
7/10

What I liked

I liked having a game where you're doing normal police work instead of constantly being involved in shootings and huge action scenes. Traffic stops, accidents, tickets and other calls give it a different type of gameplay.

What I didn't like

The jobs eventually become repetitive, and some situations start feeling more like completing a checklist than actual police work.

Combat & Gameplay

You have to pay attention to what people and vehicles are doing and decide whether there is actually a violation before taking action.

Exploration & Open World

Patrolling different parts of the city gives you some freedom to decide what situations you want to investigate.

Final Thoughts

An interesting police simulator that's fun when you want something slower and different, but the repetitive calls and activities kept it at a 7 for me.
