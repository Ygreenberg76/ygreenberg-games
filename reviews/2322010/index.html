MY REVIEW • 66.6 HOURS PLAYED

God of War Ragnarök

Story RichActionAdventure
9/10

What I liked

The story, graphics and combat are excellent. I really enjoyed seeing how Kratos and Atreus continue to change, and the game gives you much more to explore than just following the main story.

What I didn't like

Some puzzles and slower sections can interrupt the action, but they didn't stop me from really enjoying the game.

Story

The relationship between Kratos and Atreus is one of the strongest parts. The story is emotional and gives both characters room to grow.

Combat & Gameplay

Combat feels powerful and satisfying. Different weapons, abilities, armor and upgrades give you plenty of ways to fight, and the boss battles make good use of them.

Exploration & Open World

The different realms are beautiful and worth exploring. I especially liked that many side quests aren't just filler—they add more story, character development and lore.

Who I Recommend It To

Anyone who enjoys story-driven action games, great combat, exploration and strong characters.

Final Thoughts

An excellent story-driven action game with great combat, characters and exploration.
