MY REVIEW • 4.2 HOURS PLAYED

Blade & Sorcery

VRActionSingleplayer
7/10

What I liked

The VR combat is the main reason to play. Using swords and other weapons with your own movements makes fighting much more physical than a normal game.

What I didn't like

After the initial excitement of VR combat, I wanted more variety and more reasons to keep playing.

Combat & Gameplay

Experimenting with different weapons, magic and ways of fighting is where most of the fun comes from. The physics can create some entertaining and unpredictable fights.

Final Thoughts

A fun VR combat game with good physics and plenty of ways to fight. I enjoyed experimenting with the weapons, but the experience became repetitive for me.
