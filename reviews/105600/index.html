MY REVIEW • 26.3 HOURS PLAYED

Terraria

RPGStory RichAdventure
7/10

What I liked

There is a surprising amount to do. Exploring, collecting resources, crafting equipment and slowly becoming stronger gives you plenty of reasons to keep playing.

What I didn't like

For me, the amount of crafting and figuring out what to do next can become a little too much. I prefer games that give me a stronger story and a clearer direction.

Combat & Gameplay

You start with very little, but eventually you can find and create much better weapons, armor and equipment. That progression is satisfying.

Exploration & Open World

Digging underground and discovering new areas, enemies and resources is a big part of the experience. You never really know what you're going to find.

Building & Customization

There are a huge number of things you can build and create. I can see why players who really enjoy building and crafting can spend hundreds of hours with it.

Final Thoughts

A good game with a huge amount of content and freedom, but the heavy focus on crafting and building isn't as much my style as a story-driven RPG.
