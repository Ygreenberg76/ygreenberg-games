MY REVIEW • 20.9 HOURS PLAYED

Rise of the Tomb Raider

StealthStory RichAction
8/10

What I liked

I liked the combination of exploration, climbing, combat and tombs. The game gives you enough variety that you're not doing the same thing all the time.

What I didn't like

Some of the open areas contain too many collectibles and small activities. I didn't always feel the need to complete everything on the map.

Story

Lara's search for the Divine Source gives the adventure a good reason to keep moving forward, although the gameplay and exploration were stronger for me than the story.

Combat & Gameplay

Using the bow, guns, crafting and stealth gives you several ways to deal with enemies. I especially liked using the environment and stealth before starting a direct fight.

Exploration & Open World

The optional tombs are one of the best parts. Solving puzzles and reaching hidden areas makes exploration feel rewarding.

Final Thoughts

A very good action-adventure game with a nice balance between exploration, combat, climbing and puzzles.
