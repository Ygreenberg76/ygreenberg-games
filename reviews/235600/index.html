MY REVIEW • 46.3 HOURS PLAYED

Tom Clancy's Splinter Cell Blacklist

StealthActionSingleplayer
8.5/10

What I liked

I really enjoyed the stealth and the freedom to decide how I wanted to complete missions. I could try to get through without being detected or become much more aggressive when things went wrong.

What I didn't like

Sometimes the game moves more toward action than the older Splinter Cell style, and I personally enjoy Splinter Cell most when stealth is the main focus.

Combat & Gameplay

The stealth is excellent. Moving between cover, watching enemy patrols and finding the right moment to move makes missions satisfying. The game also lets you use non-lethal methods or become much more aggressive.

Building & Customization

I liked being able to upgrade equipment and choose gear based on how I wanted to approach the next mission.

Who I Recommend It To

Anyone who enjoys stealth games, tactical gameplay and having different ways to complete a mission.

Final Thoughts

One of my favorite modern Splinter Cell games because it gives you freedom without taking away the stealth.
