MY REVIEW • PLAYTIME NOT RECORDED

Scourge: Outbreak

ShooterActionSingleplayer
7/10

What I liked

I like the science-fiction setting and the focus on third-person shooting and action.

What I didn't like

The overall presentation and gameplay don't stand out as much compared with stronger third-person shooters.

Combat & Gameplay

The cover-based shooting is familiar and easy to understand, but there isn't one major gameplay mechanic that makes it completely different from similar games.

Final Thoughts

A decent science-fiction shooter that can be enjoyable without doing anything especially memorable for me. 7/10.
