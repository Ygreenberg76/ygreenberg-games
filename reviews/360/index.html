Half-Life Deathmatch: Source

ShooterActionMultiplayer
7/10

What I liked

It's simple, fast and gives you the classic Half-Life weapons in a multiplayer deathmatch environment.

What I didn't like

There isn't much here for me beyond the multiplayer combat, and that's not normally enough to keep me interested for a long time.

Combat & Gameplay

It's old-school FPS multiplayer: move quickly, learn the weapons and maps, and try to react faster than the other players.

Final Thoughts

A straightforward classic multiplayer shooter. Fun for what it is, but not something I would choose over a good single-player FPS. 7/10.
