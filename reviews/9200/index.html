MY REVIEW • 16 HOURS PLAYED

RAGE

Open WorldShooterStory Rich
7/10

What I liked

The shooting feels good, and the weapons make fighting mutants and other enemies enjoyable. The post-apocalyptic setting also gives you some interesting places to visit.

What I didn't like

The world looks like it should offer more freedom than it actually does. I wanted more exploration and more things to discover between missions.

Story

The story works well enough to move you between missions, but it wasn't the main reason I kept playing.

Combat & Gameplay

Gunplay is definitely the strongest part. The weapons feel powerful, and fighting aggressive mutants can become intense when they start getting close to you.

Exploration & Open World

There is driving and an open-world structure, but the exploration isn't as deep as I would have liked.

Final Thoughts

A good shooter with strong gunplay and an interesting post-apocalyptic setting, but I wanted more from its world and exploration.
