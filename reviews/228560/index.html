MY REVIEW • 51.3 HOURS PLAYED

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Out of the Shadows

ActionSingleplayerAdventure
7/10

What I liked

I enjoyed playing as the different Turtles and using their different attacks and abilities. The combat is probably the strongest part, especially when you start unlocking better moves and team attacks.

What I didn't like

The camera can become frustrating during fights, and the game has technical problems that sometimes hurt what should be simple fun.

Combat & Gameplay

The combat reminds me a little of the Batman Arkham style, with combos, counters and fighting groups of enemies. Switching between the Turtles also gives you some variety.

Who I Recommend It To

TMNT fans who want a simple action game and enjoy beat-'em-up combat.

Final Thoughts

Not an amazing game, but as a TMNT fan there is still fun to be had with the Turtles and the combat.
