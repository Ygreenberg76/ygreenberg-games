MY REVIEW • 1.317 HOURS PLAYED

Quaver

SingleplayerActionAdventure
7/10

What I liked

The rhythm gameplay is simple to understand but becomes much more challenging when the songs get faster.

What I didn't like

Rhythm games aren't really the type of game I normally spend a lot of time playing, so I didn't get deeply into it.

Combat & Gameplay

Timing your inputs correctly with the music is the entire challenge. Improving your timing and trying to get a better score can become addictive.

Final Thoughts

A good rhythm game that does what it's supposed to do. I had some fun with it, but it's not really the type of game that keeps me playing for a long time.
