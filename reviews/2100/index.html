MY REVIEW • PLAYTIME NOT RECORDED

Dark Messiah of Might & Magic Single Player

ShooterActionSingleplayer
8/10

What I liked

The first-person combat and fantasy setting work well together, especially because you can use weapons, magic and the environment during fights.

What I didn't like

The game definitely shows its age in its graphics, controls and presentation.

Combat & Gameplay

Being able to interact with the environment during combat makes fights more entertaining. It isn't just about repeatedly attacking an enemy with the same weapon.

Final Thoughts

An older game with some very interesting combat ideas. I like the combination of first-person action, fantasy and environmental combat enough to give it an 8.
