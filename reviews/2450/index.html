MY REVIEW • 0.117 HOURS PLAYED

Bloody Good Time

ActionMultiplayerSingleplayer
5/10

What I liked

The concept is different, with a more humorous style than a typical multiplayer action game.

What I didn't like

I only played for a few minutes, and nothing about the gameplay made me want to continue for much longer.

Combat & Gameplay

There are different weapons and ways to attack other players, but I didn't spend enough time with it to learn the systems properly.

Final Thoughts

An interesting idea, but my first impression wasn't strong enough to keep me playing. With such limited playtime, a 5 feels right for my experience.
