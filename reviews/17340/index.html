MY REVIEW • PLAYTIME NOT RECORDED

Crysis Wars

ShooterStory RichMultiplayer
8/10

What I liked

I liked having the Crysis weapons, nanosuit abilities and larger environments available in a multiplayer-focused game.

What I didn't like

As a multiplayer experience, it doesn't have the story and progression that normally keep me interested in a game.

Combat & Gameplay

The nanosuit gives the shooting more options because movement, armor and other abilities can change how you approach another player.

Final Thoughts

A good extension of the Crysis gameplay into multiplayer. I prefer having a full single-player experience, but the underlying gameplay is still good enough for an 8.
