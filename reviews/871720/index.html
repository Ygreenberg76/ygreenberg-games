MY REVIEW • 3.3 HOURS PLAYED

Ultimate Custom Night

SingleplayerActionAdventure
7/10

What I liked

I liked how much control the game gives you over the difficulty. Having so many different animatronics to choose from lets you create your own challenge.

What I didn't like

The gameplay becomes extremely difficult and sometimes feels like you're trying to manage too many things at the same time.

Combat & Gameplay

You constantly have to watch different systems and remember how each animatronic works. Increasing the number and difficulty makes the game much more stressful.

Final Thoughts

A good challenge for Five Nights at Freddy's fans with a huge amount of customization. I enjoyed trying different combinations, but the gameplay can become overwhelming.
