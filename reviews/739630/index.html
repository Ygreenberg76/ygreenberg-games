MY REVIEW • 22.9 HOURS PLAYED

Phasmophobia

HorrorSingleplayerAction
7/10

What I liked

Trying to figure out what type of ghost you're dealing with can be fun, especially when strange things start happening and you're trying to collect enough evidence.

What I didn't like

The basic gameplay becomes repetitive. You enter a location, search for evidence, identify the ghost and repeat the process again.

Combat & Gameplay

Using different equipment to look for evidence gives you something to think about instead of simply walking around waiting for a ghost to appear.

Exploration & Open World

The atmosphere is probably the strongest part. Dark buildings, strange noises and not knowing when something is going to happen create a lot of tension.

Final Thoughts

A good horror game with an interesting idea, but after enough hours the investigations started feeling too similar for me.
