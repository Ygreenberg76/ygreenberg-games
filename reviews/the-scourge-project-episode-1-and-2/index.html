MY REVIEW • 0.75 HOURS PLAYED

The Scourge Project: Episode 1 and 2

ShooterActionSingleplayer
5/10

What I liked

The science-fiction setting and third-person shooting gave me some interesting ideas to work with during the short time I played.

What I didn't like

The game felt dated and didn't give me enough reason to continue playing. The controls and overall presentation didn't really pull me in.

Combat & Gameplay

The shooting works, but from my limited experience there wasn't anything about the combat that made it stand out from other third-person shooters.

Final Thoughts

I only played for about 45 minutes, but it didn't make a strong enough first impression for me to continue. There are some decent ideas here, but it's a 5 for me.
