MY REVIEW • 6.4 HOURS PLAYED

Feed and Grow: Fish

SurvivalSingleplayerAction
6/10

What I liked

Starting as a small fish and gradually becoming larger by eating other creatures is simple but satisfying.

What I didn't like

There isn't enough variety to keep the experience interesting for a long time. Once I understood the basic gameplay loop, it became repetitive.

Combat & Gameplay

The main idea is survival and growth. You start vulnerable, eat smaller creatures and eventually become powerful enough to attack things that were dangerous earlier.

Final Thoughts

A simple and entertaining idea that can be fun for a while, but there wasn't enough depth or variety to keep me interested longer.
